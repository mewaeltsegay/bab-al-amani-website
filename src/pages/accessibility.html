Accessibility statement

We want everyone to be able to use this website, including people who use screen readers, keyboard navigation or zoom. Last reviewed 5 October 2026.

On this page Our target What we've done Known limitations Report a problem

Our target

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA, the standard behind the European Accessibility Act and EN 301 549.

What we've done

  • Text and background colours meet WCAG AA contrast. Small green text uses a darker shade (#1F7A50) for that reason.
  • Every page works with a keyboard, has a visible focus outline and a "skip to content" link.
  • Pages use proper headings, landmarks and labelled form fields, so screen readers can navigate them.
  • Images have text alternatives. The logo is labelled as a link home.
  • The layout works from 320px wide and at 200% zoom without horizontal scrolling.
  • Animations are switched off if your device asks for reduced motion.
