03 — How we work

How an order works.

Four steps. You see the full price before anything leaves Dubai.

STEP 01

Tell us what you need

Send a list, a spec sheet or a full tender pack. A photo of a handwritten list works too.

STEP 02

Get a firm quote

Price, delivery date and Incoterms, in writing. If it looks right, you sign it.

STEP 03

We source and ship

We inspect the goods, pack them, sort out the export papers and book the freight.

STEP 04

It arrives

Customs cleared and dropped at your door. Ask us where it is at any point and we'll tell you.
