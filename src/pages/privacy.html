2. Which laws apply

We follow the data protection laws that apply to us and to the people we deal with, mainly:

  • UAE Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 on the Protection of Personal Data (PDPL);
  • the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the UK GDPR, where they apply to you;
  • Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019;
  • Uganda's Data Protection and Privacy Act, 2019;
  • Tanzania's Personal Data Protection Act, 2022;
  • Rwanda's Law No. 058/2021 relating to the protection of personal data and privacy;
  • Ethiopia's Personal Data Protection Proclamation No. 1321/2024;
  • Zambia's Data Protection Act No. 3 of 2021;
  • Somalia's Data Protection Act, 2023;
  • and other local laws in the countries we deliver to, where they apply.

Where these laws differ, we apply the stricter standard where we reasonably can.

3. What personal data we collect

When you visit this website

Like any web server, our hosting provider automatically records technical data for each request: IP address, date and time, the page requested, the referring page, browser type and operating system. We use it only to deliver the site, keep it secure and fix faults. We don't combine it with other data or use it to identify you.

When you contact us or request a quote

The form on our contact page runs only in your browser. When you press send, it opens your email app with a pre-filled message. Nothing is sent to us or stored by the website. If you then send the email, we receive what you included, usually your name, company, email address, phone number, the products you need, the destination and your message.

The same applies when you email, phone or message us directly.

When you contact us on WhatsApp

Our WhatsApp links only open WhatsApp when you click them. Nothing is loaded from WhatsApp before that. If you message us there, WhatsApp (operated by Meta Platforms) processes your phone number, profile name and messages under its own terms and privacy policy, and messages are end-to-end encrypted. We use what you send us for the same purposes as an email. If you'd rather keep Meta out of it, email or call us instead.

When you become a customer, supplier or partner

We process the business contact details of the people we work with, such as names, job titles, email addresses and phone numbers. We also keep order, delivery, invoicing and payment records. Some shipments require consignee or contact details on customs and transport documents.

We don't ask for, and you shouldn't send us, sensitive data such as health information, religious beliefs or biometric data. Please don't send identity document numbers or bank card details through email or the contact form unless we have specifically asked for them for a legal requirement.

6. International transfers

We are based in the UAE, and our business moves goods between the UAE and several African countries, so personal data is necessarily transferred across borders. For example, a consignee's name and phone number appear on a bill of lading travelling from Dubai to Mombasa.

The European Commission has not issued an adequacy decision for the UAE or most of the countries we deliver to. When the GDPR or UK GDPR applies to a transfer, we rely on:

  • standard contractual clauses approved by the European Commission (or the UK International Data Transfer Addendum) with service providers; or
  • where that isn't possible, the transfer being necessary to perform a contract with you or at your request (GDPR Art. 49(1)(b) and (c)).

For transfers out of the UAE we follow Articles 22 and 23 of the PDPL. For transfers out of Kenya, Uganda, Tanzania, Rwanda, Ethiopia, Zambia or Somalia, we follow the cross-border transfer rules in those countries' laws. You can ask us for a copy of the safeguards we use.

8. Security

We use technical and organisational measures to protect personal data. These include encrypted connections (HTTPS) for this website, access controls and multi-factor authentication on our email and business systems, limited staff access, and confidentiality agreements. If a breach is likely to put your rights at risk, we will notify the competent authority and you as the applicable law requires.

9. Your rights

Depending on the law that applies to you, you have the right to:

  • access: ask for a copy of the personal data we hold about you;
  • rectification: have inaccurate data corrected or incomplete data completed;
  • erasure: have your data deleted when we no longer need it or have no legal basis for keeping it;
  • restriction: ask us to limit how we use your data while a question about it is resolved;
  • portability: receive data you gave us in a structured, machine-readable format, or have it sent to someone else;
  • objection: object to processing based on our legitimate interests, and to direct marketing at any time;
  • withdraw consent where we rely on consent, without affecting what we did before;
  • not be subject to decisions based solely on automated processing (we don't make any).

To use any of these rights, email contact@babalamani.com. We may need to confirm your identity first. We reply within one month, or sooner if your local law requires. If a request is complex we may extend that, as the law allows, and tell you why. Using your rights is free unless a request is clearly unfounded or excessive.

10. Complaints

Please tell us first if you're unhappy with how we've handled your data. We'd like the chance to put it right. You also have the right to complain to a data protection authority, for example:

  • UAE: the UAE Data Office;
  • Kenya: the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC);
  • Uganda: the Personal Data Protection Office (PDPO);
  • Tanzania: the Personal Data Protection Commission (PDPC);
  • Rwanda: the National Cyber Security Authority (NCSA);
  • Zambia: the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner;
  • Somalia: the Data Protection Authority;
  • EU: the supervisory authority in the country where you live or work, or where the issue arose;
  • UK: the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).

11. Cookies

This website does not set cookies or use similar tracking technologies. The only exception is the optional Google map on our contact page. It loads only after you press "Show the map", and that is your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)). From then on Google, as an independent controller, receives your IP address and may set cookies. Details are in our cookie policy.
