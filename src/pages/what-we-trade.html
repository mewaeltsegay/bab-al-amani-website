01 — Building & construction

Materials that show up before the crew does.

A site with no rebar on it still costs money every day. We plan construction orders backwards from the date you need them on site, and we tell you early if a mill or a ship is running late.

We supply full containers to contractors and mixed loads to builders' merchants. For road builders and waterproofing contractors we also supply bitumen, in drums or in bulk, in common penetration grades such as 60/70 and 80/100.

05 — Tobacco & shisha

Licensed trade buyers only.

We supply cigarettes, shisha tobacco (molasses), hookahs and shisha charcoal to licensed importers, wholesalers and duty-free operators. We don't sell to consumers, and we don't promote tobacco brands. Product lists and prices go to verified trade buyers on request.

Before we quote

  • Your company registration and a valid tobacco import or distribution licence for the destination country
  • Details of the consignee and the delivery address

On every shipment

  • Health warnings, excise or tax stamps and pack markings that the destination country requires
  • Accurate customs declarations and duties paid under the agreed Incoterm
  • A record of each consignment from manufacturer to buyer

We refuse orders that don't meet these conditions, including any request to ship undeclared or to a different destination than the one licensed.
